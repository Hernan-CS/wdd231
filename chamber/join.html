Non Profit Membership

No fee. Ideal for registered non-profit organizations. Benefits include access to community events, inclusion in the directory, and volunteer-networking opportunities.

Bronze Membership

Bronze level includes listing in the directory, invites to select events, and discounted training sessions.

Silver Membership

Silver adds highlighted placement in the member directory, advertising opportunities, priority event registration, and training access.

Gold Membership

Gold includes premium homepage spotlight positions, free or heavily discounted events, priority advertising, and tailored training and promotion packages.
